    Plan a safe Marswalk at Jezero Crater. The habitat (⌂) sits at Perseverance's landing site. Click the map to add science targets; the planner compares Fastest, Safest and Most science routes over real NASA terrain, within your EVA time and distance limits.

    How it works

    Terrain. A 20 m elevation model of Jezero (MRO CTX stereo, USGS) is turned into slope and roughness. Each 20 m cell gets a hazard class: easy <8°, moderate 8–15°, difficult 15–25°, no-go >25°.

    Routing. A* search finds the least-cost path across the grid. Cost = walking time from a Tobler hiking model (uphill and steep downhill are slower) × a hazard penalty that depends on the route profile.

    Safety. A reverse search from the habitat gives the return time from every cell. The safe-return zone shows where an astronaut can walk to and back within the EVA budget. The turn-back point is the last spot on the route where you can still get home in time.

    Assumptions: suited walking speed = 60% of Earth Tobler speed; 20 m data misses boulders <20 m. Not for real mission use.
